## 📘 What the subject covers

> **Fundamentos Matemáticos (780001)** · 6 ECTS · 1st year · 1st semester · contents from the 2024-25 teaching guide · [official subject page](https://www.uah.es/es/estudios/estudios-oficiales/grados/asignatura/Fundamentos-Matematicos-780001/)

| Unit | Hours | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| Linear algebra | 20 h | Matrices, rank and Gaussian elimination, inverses · Systems of linear equations, null space, Rouché-Fröbenius · Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, diagonalisation, quadratic forms, Jordan form · Linear optimisation: the simplex method |
| Equations, sequences and recursion | 10 h | Algebraic equations and sequences · Recursion and proof by induction · Limits and continuity |
| Calculus | 26 h | Differentiation, Rolle, mean value and Taylor theorems, optimisation · Power series and differential equations, convergence tests · Integration techniques, definite and improper integrals, applications |
